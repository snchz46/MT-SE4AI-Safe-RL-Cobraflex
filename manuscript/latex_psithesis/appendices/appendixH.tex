\section{Reward function}
\label{sec:H.1}

The reward is \textbf{identical on both tracks} and is computed over the
\textbf{ground-truth} state plus progress, and is agnostic to the observation:

\begin{verbatim}
r = w_fwd  . max(progress, 0)
  - w_ey   . |ey|
  - w_eps  . |epsi|
  - w_ds   . |d_steering|
  - w_term . [terminated_off_road]
\end{verbatim}

where \code{progress} is the \textbf{normalised} advance along the centre
line, $\Delta$steering is the change in the \textbf{raw steering of the policy}
(not the post-cage one), and \code{[terminated\_off\_road]} is 1 only if the
episode ends by leaving the \textbf{road} (not by a \cagerule{05} emergency).
The nominal weights (subject to experimental tuning) are:

\begin{table}[t]
  \caption{Nominal reward weights.}
  \label{tab:H.1}
  \centering\small
  \begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}L{34mm}rY@{}}
    \toprule
    \tabhead{Parameter} & \tabhead{Value} & \tabhead{Rationale}\\
    \midrule
    \code{w\_fwd} (\code{forward\_progress}) & 1.0  & Rewards real progress ($\approx1.0$/step at cruise)\\
    \code{w\_ey} (\code{lateral\_error})     & 2.5  & Main penalty: lateral offset\\
    \code{w\_eps} (\code{heading\_error})    & 0.75 & Secondary penalty: heading\\
    \code{w\_ds} (\code{steer\_delta})       & 0.20 & Actuation smoothness (over the \textbf{raw} $\Delta$steering, v1.2)\\
    \code{w\_term} (\code{termination})      & 25.0 & Discourages leaving the road\\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabularx}
\end{table}

The forward term uses \textbf{normalised progress} (not speed): since the
speed is fixed, a term \code{w\_fwd}$\cdot$\code{speed} would be a constant that does not
discriminate between behaviours and left \code{explained\_variance} $\approx0$ (F3 review,
first run). The high termination penalty (25.0) prioritises staying on the
\textbf{road}; \textbf{only} the road departure applies it --- the \cagerule{05}
emergency ends the episode with no penalty (the intervention of the cage is dynamics, not
punishment; \dec{34}).\sidenote{The weights are \code{[provisional, \met{P1}..\met{P4}]}; detail in \file{docs/10\_reward\_function.md}.}

The \textbf{four stability levers} of the E-main run (\code{target\_kl}, linear LR
anneal, \code{VecNormalize(norm\_reward)} and \code{clip\_range\_vf}) do \textbf{not} exist in the
F baseline: they were added after observing that PPO over a CNN with visual
randomization is markedly less stable than over the state vector.
\code{norm\_obs} is kept \textbf{False}, so that evaluation/inference is not
affected and \code{ep\_rew\_mean} in the curve stays \textbf{raw} (comparable with the
baseline). The camera budget is $\ge$ 1M steps (\dec{41} accepts the higher
data demand of the end-to-end approach); a pilot of ${\sim}$20k validates the loop
before committing the budget.
