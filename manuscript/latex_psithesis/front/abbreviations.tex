\paragraph{Framework identifiers} The work uses a single, non-reusable identifier space, summarised here; the corresponding registers are \Cref{app:A} (hazards), \Cref{app:B} (requirements), \Cref{app:E} (cage) and \Cref{app:F} (traceability): \hz{XX} (hazard), \sr{XXX} (safety requirement), \code{SR\nbh CL\nbh A} / \code{SR\nbh CL\nbh B} (criticality class of a requirement), \cagerule{XX} (cage rule), \scn{*} (scenario), \met{*} (metric), \dec{NN} (recorded design decision), \phase{F\nbh X} / \phase{G\nbh X} (project phase and gate), \code{ODD\nbh n.PARAMETER} (declared parameter of the operational domain), \code{TBD\nbh Qn} (open question pending closure).

\paragraph{Argument identifiers} Distinct from the previous ones, these organise the thread of the text rather than the chain of evidence: \code{OE1}--\code{OE7} (specific objectives, \Cref{sec:1.4}), \code{H1}--\code{H3} (hypotheses, \Cref{sec:1.5} --- not to be confused with \hz{01}--\hz{12}, which are hazards), \code{A1}--\code{A5} (adaptations to the V-Model, \Cref{sec:3.4}), \code{R1}--\code{R14} (results, \Cref{ch:12}), \code{T1}--\code{T7} (lines of future work, \Cref{ch:12}).
