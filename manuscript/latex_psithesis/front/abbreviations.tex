\paragraph{Framework identifiers} The work uses a single, non-reusable identifier space, defined in Appendix A: \hz{XX} (hazard), \sr{XXX} (safety requirement), \cagerule{XX} (cage rule), \scn{*} (scenario), \met{*} (metric), \dec{NN} (recorded design decision), \phase{F\nbh X} / \phase{G\nbh X} (project phase and gate).
