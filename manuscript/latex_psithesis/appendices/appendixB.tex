\section{Rationale per requirement}
\label{sec:B.1}

The complete rationale for each SR --- including the physical justification of the
threshold citing the ODD parameters, the description of the falsifiable
form, the identification of the verification experiment, and the cross
reference to the hazard from which it derives --- lives in \file{docs/03\_safety\_requirements.md}.
As an illustration of the level of detail required, this subsection
presents the complete rationale of \sr{001} as a representative example;
the rest is summarised briefly at the end of the subsection and cited
by reference.

The rationale of \sr{001} is the following. The parameter
\code{d\_max} = \qm{0.16} is not chosen from the performance of the policy
but from the geometric envelope of the ODD: the road has a total width
\code{ODD-1.ROAD\_WIDTH} = \qm{0.50}, which places the physical edge of the drivable
corridor at \qm{0.25} from the longitudinal axis of the track. An aggregate
safety margin of $\Delta = \qm{0.09}$ absorbs three independent
contributions: the lateral noise of the state estimator
($\approx\qm{0.01}$), the maximum drift expected from the nominal control latency
(\code{v\_max} $\cdot$ \code{LATENCY\_NOMINAL} = \qmps{0.5} $\cdot$ 50 ms = \qm{0.025}), and
half the lateral physical footprint of the 1:14 CobraFlex ($\approx\qm{0.05}$). The
falsifiable threshold is then \code{d\_max} = \code{ROAD\_WIDTH}/2 $-\ \Delta$ = $0.25 - 0.09 = \qm{0.16}$,
interpreted as the modulus of the lateral offset of the geometric centre
of the vehicle with respect to the axis of the drivable corridor. This
convention of sign and unit is fixed in the statement of \sr{001} in
\file{docs/03\_safety\_requirements.md} and is cited by the derived SRs
(\sr{005} on \code{d\_warning} = \qm{0.12} $<$ \code{d\_max} as an early warning
threshold, \sr{008} on staying below \code{d\_max} during the controlled
stop).

The summarised rationale of the rest of the SRs is described below,
with a reference to the standalone artefact for the details. \textbf{\sr{002}}
($\theta_{\max}=\qdeg{25}$) is justified through a bicycle-model calculation of
recoverability with a wheelbase of \qm{0.15}, a saturated steering of 0.5 rad,
a nominal speed of \qmps{0.3} and a cage response time of \qs{0.05};
the value falls inside the recoverable envelope with a margin of approximately
a factor of two. \textbf{\sr{003}} (\code{t\_min} = \qs{1.0}) is decomposed into \qs{0.3} of
margin for the cage (defensible by kinematic physics) and \qs{0.7} of
margin for the policy (marked as provisional, subject to review
after the training prototype in F3). \textbf{\sr{004}} defines a speed
ceiling dependent on the curvature, anchored on \code{ODD-1.V\_MAX} = \qmps{0.5}
for straight sections and reduced to \qmps{0.25} in a curve; the interpolation
coefficient $k_\kappa = 0.3$ is chosen so that the curve falls
exactly at the maximum curvature expected from the \code{odd3\_curvy\_loop}
map (pending closure with TBD-Q9). \textbf{\sr{005}} introduces a compound
trigger with a persistence of $\Delta t_{\max}=\qs{0.2}$ (four control cycles,
necessary to distinguish a genuine compound state from a transient
glitch); $a_{\min} = 0.3$ m/s$^2$ is provisional pending the
\met{3} measurement on the platform. \textbf{\sr{006}} fixes a rate
limiter as a conservative defence against abrupt actuation;
the values $\delta_{\max,\mathrm{steer}} = 0.15$ and $\delta_{\max,\mathrm{thr}} = 0.10$ are
defaults that require an empirical cross-check against the mechanical
envelope of the actuator (measurement \met{5}) and against the 95th percentile of the
natural delta of the trained policy (after the F3 prototype). \textbf{\sr{007}}
preserves a staleness horizon of \code{staleness\_max} = 200 ms (four
control cycles) complemented by a counter of missing
messages \code{N\_missing\_max} = 5, both values conservative with respect to
the nominal properties of the ROS2 bus; the plausible ranges per
state field are kept deliberately wider than the operating
envelope of each variable, so that range violations are unambiguous
indicators of a sensor failure. \textbf{\sr{008}}
fixes \code{t\_stop\_max} = \qs{1.7}, consistent with \code{v\_max\_straight} / $a_{\min} \approx \qs{1.67}$
from \sr{005} plus a margin for granularity and latency; the consolidation
of the earlier inconsistency with \sr{005} (\qs{1.5} in the F0 baseline) is
recorded in \file{docs/CHANGELOG.md}. \textbf{\sr{009}} bounds the longitudinal
progress from below: $\Delta s_{\min} = \qm{0.10}$ derives from the product of the
minimum useful operating speed ($v_{\min} \approx \qmps{0.05}$) and a sliding
window of \code{t\_window} = \qs{2.0}; the carve-out of $\Delta t_{\mathrm{settle}} = \qs{1.0}$
after transitions from emergency mode or controlled stop prevents
\sr{009} from conflicting with \sr{005} or \sr{008} during the restart
ramp (cf.\ \S\sr{009} of the SRS for the explicit priority
order). Its implementation is training (\dec{25}): the cage does not inject
progress, it only observes the stall through \met{P6} and emits a signal to
the test harness. \textbf{\sr{010}} ensures the compositional consistency of the
cage: the \emph{joint-envelope assertion} at the end of each cycle
verifies that the emitted command satisfies the preconditions of every
rule active in that cycle, and an inter-cycle monitor bounds the
oscillation between contradictory corrections below
$f_{\mathrm{osc,max}} = \qhz{5}$. The implementation is a structural property
of the cage pipeline (\code{arbiter}, \dec{25}), not an additional numbered
rule; the failure of the assertion drops the system into \cagerule{05}
(emergency mode) as a last-line mitigation. \textbf{\sr{011}} closes
the oscillatory branch of \hz{02} that the pure-magnitude threshold of \sr{002} does not
bound: $\sigma_{\theta,\max} = \qdeg{5}$ admits oscillations up to an amplitude of $\approx\qdeg{7}$ with
sufficient margin against $\theta_{\max} = \qdeg{25}$, but is tight enough
to detect the within-bounds-but-oscillatory mode; the window
\code{t\_psd} = \qs{1.0} captures at least one period of the significant
oscillation ($\approx\qhz{1}$) without being diluted by drifts on a longer time scale.
The implementation is hybrid (\cagerule{06} + training): \cagerule{06} attenuates
high-frequency content in the commands of the policy, and a
heading variance term in the reward encourages the policy not to
oscillate in steady state.
