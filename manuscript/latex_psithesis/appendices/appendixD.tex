\section{Open questions of the domain and their closure}
\label{sec:D.1}

closed with an explicit value and its date; the twelfth remains open because of a hardware
dependency and not because of an omission.

The figure would close with \met{4}; it is carried as future work, and this spec stays below v1.0 by design.\\
