# Manuscript

The thesis manuscript, its figures, and the submission build.

## Two sources, two purposes

| Path | Role |
| --- | --- |
| `chapters/` | **Working source.** The twelve full chapters, where research writing happens. No length constraint; carries the complete argument, all evidence tables and the editorial notes in HTML comments. |
| `draft_v5/` | **Submission source.** The condensed body plus front matter and appendices, laid out to the HS Esslingen *Thesis Writing Guidelines*. Built to `.docx` by `tools/build_thesis_docx.py`. |
| `figures/` | Figures referenced from both. `figures/auto/` holds renders regenerated by the plotting tools. |

`draft_v5/` is **derived from** `chapters/` but is not generated from it: the condensation is
editorial, not mechanical. When a result changes, update `chapters/` first (it is the research
record) and then carry the change into `draft_v5/body/`.

```text
draft_v5/
  front/   cover, declaration of authenticity, abstract, preface, TOC, list of figures, abbreviations
  body/    the twelve chapters, condensed to the 80-100 page limit
  back/    bibliography (0_*) followed by appendices A-I
```

## Building the submission document

```bash
python tools/build_thesis_docx.py --out "B:/SE4AI/Documentos/draft_V5.docx"
python tools/thesis_page_budget.py "B:/SE4AI/Documentos/draft_V5.docx"
```

The first command renders the DOCX; the second drives Word to repaginate, exports a PDF and
reports where each chapter starts and **how many pages the body occupies**. The build is
idempotent and the page budget is the acceptance check.

## Guideline compliance

The layout implements the *Thesis Writing Guidelines* directly, and the choices live in one
place (`tools/build_thesis_docx.py`, top of file):

- A4, 12 pt, **1.5 line spacing**, justified, paragraphs indented.
- Margins: left 1.5", right 1", top and bottom 1.25".
- Preliminary pages in **lower-case roman, centred at the bottom**; the title page carries no
  number. Body in **arabic, upper right**, restarting at 1, with no header or ornamentation.
- Cover with the required fields (thesis type, title, both examiners, author) and the
  **certification of authenticity** as a preliminary page.
- Abstract single-spaced and within two pages.
- **Body of the text: 80-100 pages** — currently **96**, excluding bibliography and appendices.

Two guideline items are deliberately deviated from, and the reason is recorded here: the
recommendation of "three or four blank lines before subheadings" is implemented as paragraph
spacing rather than literal blank lines (visually equivalent, cheaper in pages), and citations
are parenthetical author-year rather than footnotes, which the guidelines permit.

**Open item:** the guidelines ask for standard English; the manuscript is in Spanish. Confirm
with the supervisor before final submission.

## Cross-references with the repository

Every chapter cross-references identifiers from the living documents under `docs/`. The check
that all identifiers used are defined is part of the pre-gate procedure
(`tools/check_traceability.py`).
