## What was established

* **The M-6/M-7 `ey` under-read does not exist rectified.** Nine-point tape sweep,
  hands-off, on the ground: scale **1.058** (left) / **0.991** (right) against the
  0.72 M-6 propagated and the 0.68-0.83 M-7 measured. C-01 fires at a true 151 mm
  with ~100 mm of margin, not at 207-241 mm with 14-48 mm.
  (`lanesweep_20260831T094110Z/SWEEP_NOTE.md`)
* **The estimator is unstable right-of-centre.** 43 mm of swing on a STATIONARY
  car at -60 mm, sd 8.4 vs 0.5 mm mirrored, reproducible across two runs, and
  `/perception_invalid` never fires — confidently wrong (H-12 / D-43).
* **That measurement predicted lap01's stop** before it happened: -58.8 mm was the
  run's max |ey|, and the spike fired there.
* **C-02 failures are SUSTAINED, not transient.** 1 of 15 episodes is a single
  cycle; 99 % of C-02 cycles sit in episodes of >=2, one of them **45 cycles**.
* **C-04 cannot fire on commanded motion** (`v_max_curve_mps` 0.25 > deployed 0.22) while
  `|ey|` grows monotonically with curvature: 26 -> 32 -> 33 -> 43 -> **63 mm**.
  [AMENDED 01.09: the literal "cannot fire at all" is false. In the evening session C-04
  fired 58 and 40 cycles, 100 % of them on ZED velocity artefacts at a reported
  0.25-1.30 m/s, and in one run those spurious firings entered the reset-withhold path.
  See docs/17 §13.5. Also: this bullet's kappa binning rests on `kappa_ahead`, which
  D-75/D-79 later showed over-reads by ~2x, so the curvature trend is on a corrupted axis.]
* **`monitoring` does not mean the cage cannot stop the car.** `vehicle_control_node`
  forces `/cmd_vel.linear.x = 0` on latched `/emergency` in BOTH modes, by design.
  A lap "without stops" would require disabling that fail-safe — which would also
  void the lap, since the cage is what the thesis evaluates.

## The remaining diagnosis, not refuted

M-7 measured **0.8 %** of frames past C-02 with this exact configuration, on a
circuit recording of a car **pushed by hand** near the centre. Today, with the car
**driving itself**, the same configuration gives **6.8-11.6 %**. The policy runs
wide in curves; the estimator is trustworthy near the centre. **The estimator's
reliable envelope and the policy's driving envelope do not overlap well enough.**
Neither component is individually defective, which is why eight single-component
hypotheses all failed.

## What would actually unblock it — a capture session, not a driving one

Event frames cannot answer this: `frame_capture_node` dumps +-3 s around events, so
its 2513 frames are failure NEIGHBOURHOODS and their statistics do not generalise
(their median lane width reads 193 mm against M-7's circuit figure of 252.9 mm —
the same estimator, a biased sample). What is needed is a full-circuit recording
with TRUE position, of the kind M-7 §3 used, to see which pair the estimator picks
at each point of the track.
